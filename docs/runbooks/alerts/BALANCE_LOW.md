## What it means

Available cash is below the account's low-balance threshold.

## Impact

Orders may be rejected for insufficient funds, which the OMS counts toward a reject streak and can halt the exchange.

## Diagnose

Treasury shows cash, committed capital and allocations.

## Resolve

Fund the account and record the deposit ([accounts](../procedures/accounts.md)).

## Escalate

No.
